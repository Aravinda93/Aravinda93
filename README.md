```yaml

```

- 🔭 I am part of the team that build and maintain the **[OpenEPCIS](https://github.com/openepcis)** — open-source tooling for the GS1 **EPCIS 2.0** supply-chain traceability standard.
- ⚡ I specialise in **reactive, non-blocking** services with **Quarkus** and **SmallRye Mutiny**, with strong focus on **streaming** and **memory efficiency**.
- 🌐 I also work across the stack — **Vue / Nuxt** front-ends and **AWS / Terraform** infrastructure.
- 🧩 I care about **XML ↔ JSON-LD parity**, clean code, and standards-correct implementations.
- 💬 Ask me about **EPCIS, event-hash generation, document conversion, or reactive Java**.
